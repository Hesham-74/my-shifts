How to remove your information from My Shifts.

Business owners

Open the app, go to the Staff tab, scroll to “Your account”, tap “Delete my account”, enter your password and confirm. This deletes your business, all its staff records, hours, pay, payslips, backups and every supervisor login. It cannot be undone.

Supervisors

Staff tab, “Your account”, “Delete my account”. This removes your login only; the business records stay with the owner. The owner can also remove you.

Employees

Open the staff app and tap “Remove my login”. This deletes your code, notifications and read receipts. Your hours and payslips are part of your employer’s records: to have them deleted, ask your employer.

Can’t open the app?

Email support@myshifts.ie from the email address on the account, with the business name. We will confirm and delete it within 30 days.
