My Shifts

Privacy policy

Last updated 15 November 2026

My Shifts is an app that restaurants and other businesses use to record their staff’s hours, work out pay, share rotas and send payslips. This policy explains what information the app holds, why, and what you can do about it. Questions go to support@myshifts.ie.

Who is responsible for your information

If you are an employee: your employer decides what is recorded about you (for example your hours, pay and payslips) and is responsible for it. My Shifts stores and processes that information for your employer, on their instructions. For questions about your records, ask your employer first.

If you run a business on My Shifts: My Shifts is responsible for your account details (your name, email, phone number and password) and processes your staff records for you.

What we hold

  • Business accounts: business name, owner and supervisor names, email addresses, phone numbers, and passwords (stored only as a one-way scrambled form, never in readable text).
  • Staff records your employer enters: name, position, phone number, branch, shift times, days off and leave; and, visible only to the business owner, hourly rate, bank and cash amounts, payslip figures, PPS number and email address.
  • The staff app: the code an employee chooses (stored only in scrambled form), when they last opened their times, whether they asked their manager about missing hours, and the details their phone needs to receive notifications.
  • Daily backups of each business’s records, kept so the business can recover from a lost phone.

We do not use advertising, we do not sell information, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

Why we use it

  • To run the service the business signed up for: record hours, work out pay, show staff their shifts, release payslips and send reminders.
  • To keep accounts secure, for example to send a code when someone forgets their password.

Who else handles it

We use a small number of service providers, only to run the app: Netlify (hosting and storage), Resend (sending emails such as payslips and password codes), and the notification services built into Android and iPhone (Google, Apple, Mozilla) to deliver notifications. They process information on our behalf and may store it outside Ireland under the safeguards the law requires.

How long we keep it

Records are kept while the business uses My Shifts. When a business deletes its account, its staff records, payslips, backups and logins are deleted. When an employee removes their login in the staff app, their code, notifications and read receipts are deleted; the hours and payslips belong to their employer’s records.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your information, ask us to correct it or delete it, or object to how it is used. Employees should contact their employer first; we will help your employer answer. You can also complain to the Data Protection Commission in Ireland (dataprotection.ie). Contact: support@myshifts.ie.

Children

My Shifts is for businesses and the people they employ. It is not meant for children under 16.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will say so in the app before the change applies.
